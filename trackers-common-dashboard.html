How scores work

1 · TaskOps individual

We use the saved task score events for the selected week. Green = 3 points, Yellow = 1 point, Red = 0 points.

Points score = earned points ÷ maximum points × 100. Maximum points = measured events × 3. The Green / Yellow / Red percentages show each colour’s share of those events.

Example: 9 Green + 1 Yellow = 28 / 30 points = 93.3% points score. Green rate = 90%.

TaskOps colour rules

Completion by the original due date is Green. Completion after one extension made on time is Yellow if finished by the revised due date. Late completion, a late extension, or two or more extensions is Red. Due-date-crossed and missing-due-date events are Red.

A task can generate more than one event, such as a due-date miss and later completion. An open task alone is not a completed measurement.

2 · Procurement individual

We count completed stage measurements in the selected week: Green within the stage time limit; Yellow within the next 8 working hours; Red after that.

Each colour percentage = measurements in that colour ÷ all measured stages × 100. This is timing performance; Procurement has no 3 / 1 / 0 points score.

Working calendar and stage time limits

Working hours: 9:30 am–5:30 pm IST; Sundays and second Saturdays excluded. Stage limits: Request Approval 8h, Quotation 24h, Vendor Approval 8h, PO 6h, Fund Approval 4h, Payment 16h, Dispatch 24h, QC 6h, Invoice + Accounting 8h.

Shared stage owners each receive that responsibility’s result. Imported owner attribution follows configured responsibilities; it does not prove who performed the historical action.

3 · Cumulative · common employees only

Employees match by their company email in both trackers. Add their TaskOps event counts and Procurement completed-stage counts, then calculate each colour percentage.

Example: TaskOps 9 Green out of 10 + Procurement 1 Green out of 1 = 10 ÷ 11 = 90.9% cumulative Green.

We never average the two percentages. The combined Green rate is separate from TaskOps’s points score. Both source columns remain visible.

Reading the dashboard

Workload and overdue counts show what is open now; performance uses the selected Monday–Sunday week in IST. “—” means no measurements. Source cards deduplicate Procurement stages, while employee totals can include shared responsibility.

Updates refresh when either tracker changes, with a 30-second fallback. Check the connection indicator and update time. Cumulative needs both sources. Only active admins in both trackers can see both sources; email/password or Google sign-in works.
